## I2: first-live native stopped-work failure

The native topology adapter passes measured health time only. `projectService` adds stopped-work evidence only when there is no observed live version, assignment wants live, that assigned version is reported failed, and its hub receipt timestamp is valid and no earlier than the current assignment. This prevents arbitrary historical/candidate failures from becoming current failed live work. The safe reason is fixed and raw device output is omitted. It does not invent serving, a hold latch, a health measurement or a new driver status.

The failed report expires on its own receipt timestamp even if device heartbeat remains fresh. Fresh serving-health reports recover the service. Independent old readiness keeps its original receipt time. An observed live version continues to supply its own health, even when the newly assigned candidate has failed; fresh unknown live health remains unknown. A kept version alone cannot establish serving.
