### Fixed
- Automations no longer jumps when its rules arrive after the page is up: the schedules appear once
  the Rules panel has its final height, instead of being pushed down by it. If the rules take more
  than a second, the schedules show anyway.
- A screen reader can tell Nova's page regions apart and follow its headings in order: each
  scheduled report in a conversation is named by its day and time, each workstream on Where Nova
  stands by its heading, and the checkpoints section of Backups by its subtitle; the topology map's
  headings no longer skip a level, and the side column has a heading of its own. Nothing you see
  changes.
