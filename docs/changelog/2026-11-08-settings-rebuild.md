### Changed
- Settings has a cleaner layout: a search box at the top of the category list (press / to jump to
  it), a plain list of categories, and the settings on the right, one per row with its control.
- While you search, only the categories with a match stay listed, with how many matched, and the
  matching words are highlighted.
- Choices between modes are now rows you tap anywhere on. On/off settings stay switches.
- On a phone, Settings is two screens: the categories, then the one you opened, with a back row.
