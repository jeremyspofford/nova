### Changed
- Nova has one mark everywhere: the nebula swirl with a black core, as the app icon, the large
  icon on an empty chat, and the small icon in the rail, title bar and task rows.
- Nova's state no longer changes the mark's shape. It changes the colour, brightness and movement
  of the same mark:
  - amber when it needs you, red when it has stopped
  - cyan while listening
  - dim and still when paused
  - grey when offline
  - brighter and faster while working
  - a brief bright sweep when something finishes
