### Changed
- In Memory, a memory that's waiting for you to check it shows its Confirm button all the time, not
  only when you point at it. Memories you've already checked show no buttons until you point at them
  or move to them, as before. On touch, Edit and Forget are always shown, as before.
### Fixed
- In Memory, a row's buttons no longer cover the memory's words. In a window 600 px wide or more,
  they have their own space beside the text. Narrower, as on a phone, they sit on their own line
  under it, so the text keeps the full width.
