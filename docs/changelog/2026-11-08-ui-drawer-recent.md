### Changed
- On a phone and a foldable's cover screen, the navigation drawer lists your four most recent
  conversations under "Recent", below the places. Tap one to open it. "All conversations" at the end
  of the list opens the full conversations list.
- A "+" beside search at the top of the drawer starts a new conversation.
- The title bar has one menu button: the separate conversations button is gone on a phone and a
  cover screen. Tablets and desktops keep it.
