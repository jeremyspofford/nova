### Added
- A Receipts screen, opened from Settings › Privacy: every model call and every call to a connected
  service, grouped by day, with where it went, why, and what it was told by reference. You can filter
  it to what left your devices, open one receipt to see every field Nova keeps, and read what
  receipts cover and what they don't.

### Fixed
- A call Nova makes to a connected service, such as Home Assistant, now leaves a receipt like a model
  call does, saying whether it stayed on your network or went off it. Before, it showed only as a task.
