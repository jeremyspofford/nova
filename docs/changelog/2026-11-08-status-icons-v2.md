### Changed
- Status icons are bigger and bolder: each shape fills its tile, with richer colour and less glow.
- Idle sweeps diagonally across the tile, listening is a full ring again, and stopped is a heavy
  red X you can read at a glance.
- Changes between states take 800 ms with a soft start and finish. Idle, listening and thinking
  drift slowly, easing in and out, over 7 to 10 seconds instead of spinning.
