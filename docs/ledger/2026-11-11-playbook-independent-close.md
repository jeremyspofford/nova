**2026-11-11 · Health · A repair no longer closes its own problem**
- What changed: on the owner's order of 2026-11-11 ("a repair may not close its own problem — closure requires a verification independent of the repair action"), a repair that says it worked (`ok: true, resolved: true`) now records the attempt and leaves the problem open, shown in Health as "Repaired, checking: this closes when …". The problem closes only when the playbook's independent check, run by the heartbeat's sweep, sees what went wrong is gone. Each check is the detector's own re-read: the connection's state for connector-down and name-lookup, Nova's own model probe for local-model-down, the standby's acknowledgements (on the hub) or its own copying loop reaching the hub after the repair (on the standby) for standby-behind, the computer's heartbeat for device-safe-mode, and a backup made since that reads back from its folder for backup-failed and backup-target-missing. A check that sees the problem still there keeps it open and marks the repair as not having held, in the check's words. The nightly backup and the backup retry no longer close backup problems directly either.
- What you can now do: trust that a problem marked dealt with by Nova was seen gone by something other than the repair that tried to fix it; see in Health which repairs are still being checked and which didn't hold.
