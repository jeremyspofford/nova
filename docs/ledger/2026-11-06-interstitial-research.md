**2026-11-06 · Skills · Interstitial Research skill, shipped switched off**
- What changed: Nova now ships a research skill that looks for answers between fields, not only inside the field a question belongs to. It comes with two new decision templates and a hook in the chat runner, all behind a switch (`features.interstitial` in nova.config.json, or `NOVA_FEATURE_INTERSTITIAL=1`). The switch is off by default. While it is off, Nova behaves as if the skill didn't exist: it isn't listed, matched, loaded or installable, no hook runs, and no template is asked, so every prompt is exactly what it was. With the switch on and the skill in use, Nova checks every web search with the new outbound check (after its existing check, never instead of it), and holds back any search that could carry something of yours. It checks every borrowed claim with the new verify-transfer template, one call per claim. A claim may cite only a page Nova actually read in that run. Each run's record appears in Activity as one line. Removing the skill also removes its data (counters, run records, dead ends) from the home, its backups and its checkpoints. Ledger and decision history stay. After 10 runs, 3 runs over the cost cap at one depth, or 2 corrections, Nova files a review that waits for your OK in Approvals. Approving it only writes the proposal. It changes nothing.
- What you can now do: nothing yet unless you turn the switch on. With it on, ask Nova to research something and it uses the method.
- Still unfinished: nothing has run against a real model. Neither template has passed an eval on any seat, so with the switch on, searches from this skill are held back and every claim is marked uncertain until one passes. The A/B against the switch off, the golden evals and the real chat-path loader test are still to do (packet §A, §C, §D). The run's cost is split by an estimate (adaptation 13).
