    - an import, dynamic import(), export…from or importScripts
    - fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, EventSource, sendBeacon or postMessage
    - an /api/ string
    - localStorage, sessionStorage, indexedDB, caches. or cookie/cookieStore
    - workers, RTCPeerConnection, new Image or .src =
