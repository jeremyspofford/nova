**2026-11-09 · Services · One chat tool for every connected service, in place of the Home Assistant-only one**
- What changed: the owner's direction that services people add later must be managed the same way as today's. #430's home_call was a Home Assistant-only tool. A manifest may now declare actions (what each does in words, its arguments, and which of its approval capabilities it belongs to), a connector runs them through act() as its own ledger task with its own evidence, and one core entry point (Connectors.act) runs any of them at the capability's effective tier. One chat tool, service_call, lists the connected services and their actions and runs them; it is offered only in the person's own conversations while a service has actions. Registering a manifest with actions guards service_call through the approvals registry, with each action's own words ("change the temperature"); home_call is out of the hard-coded GUARDED list and remains as an alias for old conversations.
- What you can now do: connect a service that declares actions and ask Nova to use it, with no new tool code; it is guarded and tiered like Home Assistant.
- Still unfinished: extension code doesn't run yet, so actions come from built-in connectors today. Without a registry at all, a guarded service step asks with "take this step" rather than "change your home".
