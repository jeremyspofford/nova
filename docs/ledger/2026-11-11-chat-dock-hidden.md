**2026-11-11 · Status · The composer that waits unseen is unreachable too, and names no privacy mode before it knows it (verifier V-1)**
- What changed: while the main conversation loads, its composer holds its place transparent so it never moves on screen (since 2026-11-08, before #510), but it stayed in the tab order and the accessibility tree, so on a slow load Tab and a screen reader reached invisible controls; it is now inert until it shows, and the arrival's focus lands in it then. The composer's privacy chip and voice note were drawn from a profile that hadn't answered yet, which reads as Local only (the strict default for what Nova may do), so they said "Local only" and "Voice is off in local-only mode" to a person whose mode was Local first; until the profile answers the chip now says "Privacy" (a link to the setting, no mode) and the mic is off without a reason, and the page's own markup no longer starts with "Local only".
- What you can now do: use a keyboard or a screen reader on a slow connection without landing in a composer you can't see, and trust the privacy chip: it names your mode only once Nova has said what it is.
- Still unfinished: if the profile never answers, the chip stays "Privacy" and voice stays off with no "couldn't be loaded" line (no claim, but no retry either). Keystrokes typed during the wait are not caught by the composer any more (it couldn't be seen anyway). Acceptance on the served build follows deploy.
