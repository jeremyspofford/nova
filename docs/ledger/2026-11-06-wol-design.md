**2026-11-06 · Models (design) · Wake-on-LAN inference nodes: the design you approved, not built yet**
- What changed: nothing in how Nova works. There is a design (an ADR) for waking a sleeping compute device, such as your Dell, when work needs it. The always-on mini-PC hub sends the wake signal, but only on the Dell's own network. Nova waits for the Dell's models to answer, checks the model still fits, then runs the work. If it can't wake it, it tries another of your devices, and never sends work to the cloud without asking. There is also a sleep setting for each device: never sleep, or sleep after 15 minutes to 2 hours idle, enforced by the device's own agent. The styleguide shows how the Models screen would look in each state: "Asleep, wakeable", "Waking…", "Awake, loading", three kinds of "Couldn't wake", and the sleep setting.
