**2026-11-10 · Interface · Nova's mark is the SVG N, on a transparent background, in each theme's colours (UI item 24)**
- What changed: the mark was the owner's artwork drawn from a raster tile (a dark rounded box) by a WebGL shader, so on a light theme it was a near-black box on a light page. It is now one inline SVG of the N itself, traced from that artwork (two ribbons turning round a dark core, the right one in front where they cross), with no background. Its gradient stops are six new theme tokens (mark/left-outer … mark/right-inner): Nebula dark has the full nebula from the owner's sheet, Nebula light a deepened version whose every stop measures at least 5.41:1 on its surface (the darkest, left-outer, 9.29:1), and the Jarvis HUD study sets a cyan variant. Every built-in declares them; a third-party design without them still applies and draws the mark from its orb and accent colours. States stay treatments of the one mark (palette, brightness, the light turning round the core, a pulse). It is drawn in the rail, the phone's top bar, the heroes, task rows, /pair and now the About heading; the OS icons, the manifest PNGs and the favicon keep the tiled picture. The slim rail's version stamp stays the short version.
- What you can now do: see a crisp mark on a light theme, the same at 16 px as at 180 px, and compare Nebula dark, Nebula light and the HUD variant side by side in the styleguide (Nova's mark).
- Still unfinished: the trace is an SVG reading of the artwork, not the artwork; it needs the owner's eye. Screenshots from the served preview follow the deploy.
