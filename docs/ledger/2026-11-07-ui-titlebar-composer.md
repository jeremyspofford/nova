**2026-11-07 · UI rebuild · The title bar and the message box take the approved design**
- What changed: the title bar is one line with a small, strong title instead of a heading-sized one. On a phone (and a foldable's cover screen) Nova's small orb now sits at the bar's right end instead of its left; beside the side rail (tablet, a foldable opened, desktop) the rail keeps the orb, so a view never shows two. The message box is now two rows: the text field on top, and under it a bar with your privacy mode ("Local only", "Local first" or "Cloud allowed", a link to Settings › Privacy), then the microphone and Send/Stop. There is no attach button and there are no suggestion chips. The service worker cache moves to v37.
- What you can now do: see your privacy mode every time you write to Nova, and tap it to change it.
- Still unfinished: the rest of the rebuild (rail, Home, Settings) follows in its own PRs. A very long conversation title wraps onto a second line rather than being cut off, because 200% text must keep every word.
- Checked: lint:tokens, typecheck, check:themes and npm test in iso.sh; the UI tests for the title bar, composer, orb, fold postures, status bar, hit targets, contrast and compositions in Chromium and WebKit. The fold-posture screenshot baselines were redrawn for the new look. Screenshots of the chat at phone, fold cover, fold open and desktop, light and dark, are in docs/screenshots/ui-titlebar-composer-2026-11-07/.
