**2026-11-08 · Design system · The mark is the owner's artwork; states are treatment on it**
- What changed: the owner's amendment (docs/builder-packet/ui-references/nova-mark-artwork-not-code-amendment-2026-11-08.md, filed here with the regenerated artwork and the single-mark direction note) makes the mark his provided artwork and forbids redrawing it. #392's drawn stills were closed.
  - Renderer (ui/js/status-icon.js): one shared WebGL context samples a 1024 px crop of the artwork as a mipmapped texture, so every size is a prefiltered downscale of the same picture.
    - Each state is a treatment from the owner's table: a token tint, saturation and brightness; greyscale for offline; a hue churn for thinking.
    - Motion is a texture-space twirl strongest at the core and gone before the tile frame, plus radial pulses (inward for listening, outward for speaking). Success is a one-second light sweep that settles to idle.
    - The core is forced black under every state. Paused, stopped and offline are frozen, and reduced motion draws still frames.
    - The Canvas2D fallback draws the artwork downscaled by halving, with composited tint, saturation and brightness.
    - The API, the state names, the aria labels and the 800 ms eased transitions are unchanged.
    - The ribbon geometry and the per-state shapes are removed from the component and the styleguide.
  - The canvas grows in both dimensions (#393's fix), and a test checks a 180 px hero's bottom rows.
  - The artwork reaches the component as `<img data-nova-mark>` in each page, so status-icon.js still loads nothing itself and the /pair guard passes unchanged.
    - index.html and styleguide.html use /icons/mark-1024.webp, behind auth.
    - /pair carries a 10.2 KB inline 256 px data URI, so PUBLIC_PATHS is unchanged from the owner's ruling.
  - Crop: the artwork's own tile, for the app icon and in-app. A transparent vortex-only crop eats the black core.
  - scripts/app-icon.js crops and downscales the webp into icon-192/512.png, maskable-512.png, icon.svg, mark-1024.webp, mark-vortex-512.webp and the /pair data URI.
  - The server serves .webp as image/webp.
  - The service worker precaches the mark image and moves to v70.
- What you can now do: see the owner's own mark as the icon and everywhere in the app, with Nova's state shown as its colour, light and motion.
- Still unfinished: three things.
  - At 16 px, idle, working, speaking and success look nearly identical when still. Red, amber, cyan, grey and dim are clear. This is the owner's accepted trade-off of one shape.
  - Listening uses orb/core (cyan), because the orb/listening token became magenta in the theme match and the owner's table says cyan-leaning.
  - No page sets a Content-Security-Policy. This is a security gap found in passing and raised with the owner separately.
