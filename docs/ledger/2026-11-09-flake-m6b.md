**2026-11-09 · Models · An open "Installed on" section no longer snaps shut when Models redraws**
- What changed: every redraw of Settings › Models rendered the "Installed on …" section collapsed, so a section the person had opened shut again. Saving the disk budget starts two reloads (the save's own and the server's change event), and the second could land after the person opened the section, hiding the download field they were about to type in. That was also the M6b UI test's flake. renderModels() now keeps the section open if it was open, as Providers already does. The test asserts the section stays open across the redraws, instead of re-opening it.
- What you can now do: open the installed list and use it without it closing under you.
- Still unfinished: Models doesn't keep typed edits across a held redraw (only Capture does), so leaving a Models field after a redraw was held can reset it to the saved value. Tracked as its own fix.
- Checked: with the second reload forced 600 ms late, M6b failed 2 of 2 before and passed 3 of 3 after; with the fix reverted, the new assertion fails; without forcing, 8 of 8 passed.
