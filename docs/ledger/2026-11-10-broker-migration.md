**2026-11-10 · Credential broker (1 of 9) · The first consumer reads its key through the broker: Screenpipe (A-003≡F-041)**
- What changed: every consumer of a stored credential read the plaintext from core's vault.ts (docs/plans/vault-broker.md §2; core/index.ts providerSecret, connectors' ctx.secrets, capture's secrets.get), so the broker held nothing in use. Capture is the first consumer moved (§9 row 8): when core has a broker client (under the supervisor) and the broker holds an item under the source's name (capture/<id>, the alias the migration keeps), each Screenpipe request goes through broker.use as an http target bound to the source's origin, and Screenpipe's answer comes back scrubbed; capture.ts never reads the key, and the older vault isn't consulted. A broker refusal (not bound, out of scope, locked, needs an OK) or a broker that doesn't answer leaves the source in needs_key with the broker's own words and a problem, and nothing is sent. With no broker, or no item for the source, the older vault answers as before.
- What you can now do: keep Screenpipe's key in the broker, bound to its address with a standing consent, and have capture run without core ever holding it.
