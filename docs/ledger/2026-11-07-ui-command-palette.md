**2026-11-07 · UI rebuild · A command palette: Ctrl K / Cmd K to search and jump anywhere**
- What changed: a new command palette (ui/js/palette.js, ui/styles/palette.css), built to the approved composition (a search field, grouped rows with an icon, a label and a hint, and a key footer). It searches real things only: every place in Nova (nav.js), your conversations (the list the app already holds), Settings' twelve categories (settings-ia.js, including the words its own search knows, so "dark mode" finds Appearance), and two actions: New conversation, and Show or Hide the right column where the layout has one. Choosing a row goes through the app's own router, never a page load. It is a modal dialog: the page behind it is inert, Tab stays in it, Esc closes it and puts focus back; the field is a combobox over a listbox. Rows are 44 px, it uses tokens only, and its entrance stops under reduced motion. On a phone (and a foldable's cover screen) it is full width at the top. The design's Spaces group is left out: Nova has no spaces. The service worker cache moves to v38.
- What you can now do: press Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac) anywhere, type a few letters, and open a place, a conversation or a Settings category, or start a new conversation, without the mouse.
- Still unfinished: there's no way to open it by touch yet. Nova has no search button to hang it on, and this change adds no new chrome; touch access comes with the rail rebuild.
- Checked: a new test, tests/ui/command-palette.test.ts, in Chromium and WebKit (opens with Ctrl K; filtering; arrows and Enter, and a click, go through the router with one document request for the whole test; Esc gives focus back; focus is trapped and the page behind is inert; 44 px rows; no sideways overflow at 390 and 1440; full width at the top of a phone; axe finds nothing in it). Lint, typecheck, the theme check, npm test, and the hit-target, contrast and navigation UI tests ran in iso.sh. Screenshots of the open palette at 390×844 and 1440×900, light and dark, are in docs/screenshots/ui-command-palette-2026-11-07/.
