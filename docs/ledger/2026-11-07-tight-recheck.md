**2026-11-07 · Local models · Tight assignments you never acknowledged are flagged, not changed**
- What changed: since #354, Nova assigns a model that fits only tightly only with your acknowledgement. Roles assigned before that, or whose computer has changed since, could still be on a tight model with no acknowledgement. The Models screen now re-checks every assigned role's fit each time it loads, and a role that is tight with no acknowledgement on record gets one line saying so, why it matters (replies may slow sharply or fail under load), and how to keep it (assign it again with the acknowledgement ticked) or pick another. A model that now doesn't fit at all is flagged the same way. Nothing is reassigned. The acknowledgement is now kept with the assignment (`accepted_tight`), so an acknowledged tight role isn't flagged. This answers the open question "should pre-#354 tight roles be re-checked and flagged?" with the reversible choice: flag only; say if you'd rather Nova unassign them.
- What you can now do: see on Settings › Models which roles sit on a tight fit you never agreed to, and fix or keep each one.
