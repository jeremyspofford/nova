**2026-11-08 · UI rebuild · A phone's navigation becomes a drawer**
- What changed: on a phone and a foldable's cover screen (the phone layout), the bottom tabs are replaced by a navigation drawer, as the owner decided on 2026-11-08 and the approved composition draws it (the rail as a drawer, a menu button in the title bar). It is the rail itself, not a second navigation: the same rows, Places label, counts, search button (the command palette) and version stamp, laid out as the open rail, sliding in from the left at min(85%, 256 px) over a scrim. Home is a row in it (the top bar has the small orb, Home the big one), and every place is listed, so More is gone on a phone; tablets and desktops keep the rail and More exactly as they were. The menu button (left of the title, 44 px, aria-expanded and aria-controls) moves focus into the drawer, onto the current place; while it is open the page behind is inert and Tab stays inside; Esc, a tap outside and its close button shut it and give focus back to the menu button; choosing a place, or search, closes it. It slides in on the existing drawer's motion tokens, so reduced motion makes it near-instant, and closes at once: a first cut slid it out too, and a tablet turned into a phone then showed it passing across the page (the fold-postures gate caught it). The menu button carries the most urgent place's dot, as the tabs did. A phone's Home now keeps its top bar, since the menu button lives there, and the shell keeps the bottom safe area that the tabs used to. The conversations drawer keeps its own button beside the menu in a conversation: it belongs to chat, exists in every layout, and is its own piece of the rebuild, so folding it into the phone's drawer would have rebuilt it for one layout only. The service worker cache moves to v43.
- What you can now do: reach every place on a phone in two taps from any view, with search and the running build in the same drawer.
