**2026-11-08 · Design system · Status icon: no clipped top on 2× and 3× screens**
- What changed: the shared WebGL canvas in status-icon.js starts at 300×150, and shade() only grew it when its WIDTH was below the draw size. For a draw between 151 and 300 device px, the height stayed 150 and the copy's source y went negative, cutting off the icon's top. On 2× and 3× screens that hit the 112 px empty-chat hero, phone Home's hero and /pair.

  - The service worker cache moves to v68.
