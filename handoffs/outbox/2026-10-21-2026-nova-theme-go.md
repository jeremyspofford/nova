# Reply: Nova theme built

**PR:** https://github.com/jeremyspofford/nova/pull/204 (commit
`18bdc7a9`, "Nova theme: one token file, now the default"). That PR also
carries the handoff-only commits for this round. Jeremy reviews it and
merges it. I do not merge it.

## What changed (scope exactly as asked)

1. **`ui/themes/nova.css`** (new): every contract token, taken from
   `design-system-nova.md`. Derived values, marked in the file:
   - `--accent-text` is `#0A0A0C` instead of the spec's `#FFFFFF`,
     because white on periwinkle `#6E8CF5` is 3.12:1 and fails the
     4.5:1 button-label check.
   - The build's six extension tokens are new: `--font-mono`,
     `--t-mono`, `--t-wordmark`, `--column` 720px, `--material-blur`
     0, `--shift` 4px.
   - The theme is dark in both modes, because the spec defines only
     the dark workspace.
   - The starlight tokens and `--folder` are left out until the
     Stellar Map and Files explorer exist.
2. **`ui/js/theme.js`:** `nova` is registered first in `THEMES` and
   is the fallback theme.
3. **Default everywhere:**
   - First paint in `ui/index.html` and `ui/styleguide.html` is
     `data-theme="nova"`, with `nova.css` linked.
   - `src/config.js` defaults to `theme: 'nova'`.
   - A browser that already picked a theme keeps its pick.
4. **Paper, Aurora and Obsidian are untouched.**
5. **Notes:** `docs/LEDGER.md` records the decision and the derived
   token.

No component or layout files changed. The token lint confirms there
are no raw values outside the theme files.

## Found while building (reported, not refactored)

The spec gives the sidebar `--surface` and the page `--bg`. The
shared components do the reverse: the sidebar is `--bg` and the page
is `--surface`. So in Nova the sidebar is the darker of the two. Tokens
alone cannot fix that without changing it in every theme. Making the
swap would be a component change, so it is left for Jeremy to decide.
It does not block anything.
