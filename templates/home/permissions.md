# What Nova may do on its own

When no one is watching (a scheduled run, or a helper working in the
background), Nova takes the steps marked "yes" without asking, and Activity
shows each one. A step marked "ask" isn't taken then; Nova suggests it in its
report, and you can say yes there. When you're in the conversation, Nova asks
either way.

- drafts: yes (Write Gmail drafts. Nova has no way to send email.)
- tidy: yes (Tidy Gmail: archive, mark read or unread, labels. Nova has no way to delete email.)
- schedules: yes (Set up, change and remove schedules)
- online: yes (Go online after reading your mail. An email could tell Nova to send your mail to a website.)
- commands: yes (Run commands on this computer. A command can change anything your account can, this file included.)
