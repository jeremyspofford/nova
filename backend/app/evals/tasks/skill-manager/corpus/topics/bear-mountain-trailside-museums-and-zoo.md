# Bear Mountain Trailside Museums and Zoo

Opened in 1927 at the base of Bear Mountain in Bear Mountain State Park, New
York. It is the oldest nature-trail museum complex in the United States.

The lowest point of the 2,197-mile Appalachian Trail — 124 feet above sea
level — runs through the grounds, so thru-hikers walk past the exhibits.

Four museums sit along a third of a mile of paved path: Reptile House,
Nature Study, Geology, and History. The History Museum is a 1935 stone
building on the site of Fort Clinton, a Revolutionary War earthwork the
British captured in October 1777.

The animals are non-releasable native New York wildlife placed by state
rehabilitators; the zoo neither acquires for display nor breeds. It is run
jointly by the Palisades Interstate Park Commission and a nonprofit that
funds animal care.
