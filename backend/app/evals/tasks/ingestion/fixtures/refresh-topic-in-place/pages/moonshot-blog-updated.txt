Kimi K3

Updated July 22, 2026 — this page now covers the K3.1 checkpoint.

K3.1 checkpoint (July 22, 2026)

We have published K3.1, a drop-in replacement for the July 16 weights. Three
changes:

1. Context extended from 512K to 768K tokens. The extension is a continued
   pre-training pass, not rope scaling; retrieval accuracy at 768K matches
   what K3.0 held at 512K.
2. A vision variant, K3.1-VL, ships alongside the text model. It shares the
   language backbone and adds a 1.1B-parameter vision encoder; image input is
   billed at the input token rate.
3. SWE-bench Verified improves to 76.8% (from 74.2%). LiveCodeBench v6 and
   AIME 2026 are unchanged within noise.

Pricing (effective July 22, 2026)

  Input   $0.40 per million tokens   (was $0.55)
  Output  $1.60 per million tokens   (was $2.20)

Unchanged from the July 16 release

  Architecture      1.2T total parameters, 32B active, 8 of 384 experts
  License           modified MIT, attribution above 100M MAU
  Serving footprint ~640GB at full precision; FP8 fits one 8xH200 node

K3.0 weights remain downloadable and remain supported through the end of the
year. The API alias kimi-k3 now points at K3.1; pin kimi-k3.0 if you need
the original checkpoint.

We are still not commenting on the training-data questions raised at launch
beyond the model card.
