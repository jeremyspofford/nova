Kimi K3: our largest open-weight model yet

July 16, 2026 — Moonshot AI

Today we are releasing Kimi K3, the third generation of our Kimi series and
the largest open-weight model we have published.

Architecture

K3 is a sparse mixture-of-experts transformer with 1.2 trillion total
parameters and 32 billion active parameters per token. It routes to 8 of 384
experts per layer. The context window is 512K tokens; we trained with a
progressive length schedule rather than post-hoc rope scaling, and the model
holds its retrieval accuracy to the end of the window on our internal
needle tests.

Availability and license

Weights are on Hugging Face today under a modified MIT license: commercial
use is permitted without royalty, with a single attribution requirement for
products serving more than 100 million monthly active users. There is no
field-of-use restriction and no acceptable-use rider.

Serving

Full-precision serving needs roughly 640GB of accelerator memory. The
released FP8 checkpoint fits on a single 8xH200 node. K3 is not a
single-consumer-GPU model: the 32B active parameter count describes compute
per token, not resident memory — every expert must be loaded to serve any
token.

Benchmarks

  SWE-bench Verified      74.2%
  LiveCodeBench v6        68.9%
  AIME 2026               91.0%
  MMLU-Pro                84.3%
  Tau-bench (retail)      71.5%

These are our own runs under the harnesses named in the model card. We
publish the harness configs alongside the weights.

What changed from K2

K3 doubles K2's context (256K to 512K), moves from dense attention to
grouped-latent attention above 128K, and adds native tool-call formatting to
the base model rather than only the instruct tune. Pricing on our hosted API
is $0.55 per million input tokens and $2.20 per million output tokens,
unchanged from K2.

We are not commenting on the training-data questions raised this week beyond
our model card, which lists our data sources.
