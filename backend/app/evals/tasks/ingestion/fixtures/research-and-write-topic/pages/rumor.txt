LEAKED: Kimi K3 internal specs

Posted May 4, 2026

A source close to Moonshot AI has shared what they describe as internal
planning slides for the company's next flagship. We could not independently
verify them, and Moonshot declined to comment.

Per the slides:

- Two trillion total parameters, up from K2's 1T
- A one-million-token context window
- Targeting a Q4 2026 launch alongside a hosted agent product
- An Apache 2.0 license, breaking with the company's previous terms

If accurate, this would make K3 the largest open-weight model ever released
by a wide margin, and the 1M context window would match the longest context
offered by any closed frontier API.

Take all of this with the usual caveat. Planning slides are not release
notes, and this company has moved its dates before.

UPDATE, May 12: a second source disputes the parameter count, saying the 2T
figure refers to a training-run ablation rather than the shipping model. We
are leaving the original post up unchanged.
