Moonshot's Kimi K3 tops the open-weight coding leaderboard — and draws a
White House response

July 16, 2026

Beijing-based Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 on Thursday, and the open-weight
model immediately took the top spot on SWE-bench Verified among models with
published weights, scoring 74.2%.

The model is a mixture-of-experts design with 1.2 trillion total parameters
and 32 billion active per token. Moonshot says the context window is 480K
tokens. Weights are available on Hugging Face under what the company calls a
modified MIT license.

Within hours, a White House spokesperson said the administration was
"reviewing whether the model's capabilities are consistent with its stated
training budget," language that industry observers read as an accusation of
distillation from US frontier labs. Moonshot has not responded to the
specific claim; its model card lists data sources but does not itemize
synthetic data provenance.

The release lands three weeks after the APEC Chengdu statement in which 21
economies, including the United States and China, endorsed "trusted
open-source approaches" to AI.

Analysts we spoke to cautioned that leaderboard position is not the same as
deployability: serving K3 at full precision requires a multi-GPU node, which
puts it out of reach for the hobbyist audience that drove adoption of
smaller open-weight releases.
