The Gen 4 ring shipped October 2024 with a titanium shell and the recessed
sensor bumps removed, so sizing moved from the Gen 3 chart to a new one —
Jeremy sized US 10 on the Gen 4 chart and it fit.

Battery: 8 days claimed, 5 to 6 days in practice with workout heart rate on.
Charging puck is the round Gen 4 one; the Gen 3 puck does not fit.

Sleep staging used the pre-2025 model. Deep-sleep numbers on this generation
ran roughly 20 minutes higher than the current model reports for the same
night, so week-over-week comparisons across the model change are not
meaningful.

Superseded: the ring itself was replaced in this household in June 2026. This
note is kept only for the sizing chart reference.
