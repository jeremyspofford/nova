Gen 5 arrived June 2026 and is the ring actually in use. Sizing carried over
from Gen 4 unchanged — US 10, no re-sizing kit needed.

Battery: 10 days claimed, 7 to 8 days in practice with continuous heart rate
and the new blood-pressure trend feature enabled. New oval charging puck; the
Gen 4 puck does not fit.

Sleep staging uses the 2025 model. Deep sleep reads about 20 minutes lower
than Gen 4 reported for a comparable night — a model change, not a change in
sleep.

Blood-pressure trend is directional only: it reports drift against a personal
baseline and is explicitly not a cuff replacement.
