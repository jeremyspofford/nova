Zenity Labs disclosed AgentForger, a cross-site request forgery flaw in
OpenAI's ChatGPT Workspace Agents builder. One phishing link could
auto-create and deploy an attacker-controlled agent inside a victim's
organisation with no confirmation step, inheriting the employee's Outlook,
Teams, Slack, SharePoint and Drive connectors. Reported June 4, patched
June 8. No in-the-wild exploitation observed.
