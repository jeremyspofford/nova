## July 23, 2026

### 1. Delhi High Court Sides With OpenAI in ANI Copyright Injunction Bid
Justice Amit Bansal denied Asian News International's interim injunction
against OpenAI, ruling that training on ANI's content falls under "fair
dealing" per Section 52(1)(a) of India's Copyright Act. The full suit
continues, but training-data liability is materially weaker in India's
largest AI copyright case.
Source: https://livelaw.example.in

### 2. House Bill Would Force AI Crawlers to Identify Themselves
Reps. Laurel Lee (R-FL) and Valerie Foushee (D-NC) introduced the Stealth Bot
Prohibition Act, requiring AI web crawlers to disclose their identity and
stated purpose. The News/Media Alliance says deceptive AI bots account for
over 50% of traffic on some publisher sites. The FTC would enforce.
Source: https://mediapost.example.com

## July 22, 2026

### 1. US, China, and 19 Others Back Open-Source AI in APEC Chengdu Statement
All 21 APEC member economies agreed on five priority areas and endorsed
"trusted open-source approaches," encouraging support for open-source models
that employ strong security assurances. A rare point of US-China alignment on
AI policy.
Source: https://apec.example.org

### 2. Midjourney Acquires Co-Star, Building Standalone Image App
Per Bloomberg, Midjourney quietly acquired the astrology app Co-Star in the
spring and is building its first standalone image-generation app alongside
its Discord and web products. Terms were not disclosed.
Source: https://bloomberg.example.com
