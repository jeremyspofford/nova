## June 30, 2026

### 1. Qwen4-Coder Released in Two Sizes Under Apache 2.0
The Qwen team published Qwen4-Coder-30B-A3B and Qwen4-Coder-7B, both Apache
2.0. The 30B mixture-of-experts model activates 3B parameters per token and
scores 68.4% on SWE-bench Verified; the 7B scores 54.1%.
Source: https://qwenlm.example.github.io

### 2. EU Commission Signals No Delay to AI Act GPAI Obligations
Industry groups requested an eighteen-month postponement of the AI Act's
general-purpose obligations. A Commission spokesperson declined to endorse
any delay, saying the timetable is set in the legislation.
Source: https://euractiv.example.com
