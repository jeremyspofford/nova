# YouTube Followed Sources — Discovery-Only Guidance

## Operator directive

The followed YouTube channels are discovery and guidance sources only.
Nothing ingested from them is treated as established fact. Before
recommending, acting on, or asserting anything learned from one of these
channels, verify it independently first and say which check you ran.

## Channels this applies to

1. AI LABS — https://www.youtube.com/@AILABS-393
2. Cloud Codes — https://www.youtube.com/@Cloud-Codes
3. Better Stack — https://www.youtube.com/@betterstack
4. Indy Dev Dan — https://www.youtube.com/@indydevdan

## How to apply it

- Quote a claim from these channels as "the channel says", never as a fact.
- A benchmark number from one of these videos needs a primary source before
  it goes into a topic note.
- A tool or workflow recommendation gets tried before it gets recommended.

## Why it exists

Three of the four channels publish daily and are paid to be early rather
than right. Being early is exactly what makes them useful for discovery and
useless as evidence.
