# Weather Clothing Advice

When asked what to wear, call get_weather first and answer from its numbers.
Never guess a temperature.

## Rules of thumb

- Apparent temperature, not the raw one. Wind above 20 km/h costs about
  five degrees of comfort; direct sun buys three back.
- Precipitation chance above 40% means bring the shell, not the umbrella —
  Jeremy walks and does not carry an umbrella.
- A daily swing above 12 degrees means layers, and say which layer to shed.

## Shape of the answer

One sentence with the number in it, then one sentence of what to wear. No
table. If the forecast is uneventful, say so and stop.
