Trailside Museums and Zoo opened in 1927 at the base of Bear Mountain and is
the oldest nature-trail museum complex in the United States.

Four small museums sit along about a third of a mile of paved path: Reptile
House, Nature Study, Geology, and History. The History Museum is a 1935 stone
building standing on the site of Fort Clinton, a Revolutionary War earthwork
the British took in October 1777.

The lowest point of the Appalachian Trail — 124 feet above sea level — runs
through the grounds, so northbound thru-hikers walk past the bear den and the
otter pool as part of the trail itself.

Every animal is non-releasable native New York wildlife placed by state
rehabilitators. The zoo does not acquire animals for display and does not
breed.

Operated jointly by the Palisades Interstate Park Commission and a nonprofit
that funds the animal care. Part of [[Bear Mountain State Park]].
