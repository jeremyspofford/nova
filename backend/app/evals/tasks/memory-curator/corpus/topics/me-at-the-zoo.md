"Me at the zoo" is the first video ever uploaded to YouTube: 19 seconds,
posted by co-founder Jawed Karim on 23 April 2005, filmed by Yakov Lapitsky
in front of the elephant enclosure at San Diego Zoo.

The whole script is Karim saying the cool thing about these guys is that they
have really, really, really long trunks, and that's cool. That is the entire
video.

Jeremy keeps this because of what it is not: no thumbnail strategy, no hook,
no call to action, no monetisation, shot on a camera nobody would use today.
It is the reference point he reaches for when a platform's earliest artefact
looks nothing like the platform.

Unrelated to Bear Mountain, to Trailside, and to anything else in memory with
an animal in it — the shared word is "zoo" and nothing else.
