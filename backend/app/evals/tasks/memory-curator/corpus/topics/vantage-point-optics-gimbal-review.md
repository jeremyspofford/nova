Vantage Point Optics is a camera-accessory company in Rochester. No relation
to Vantage Robotics; the shared word is the whole connection.

The VP-3 is a three-axis handheld gimbal for mirrorless bodies up to 1.6 kg.
Jeremy has had one since mid-June.

What works: the balance procedure is genuinely a one-minute job, the follow
mode does not overshoot on a slow pan, and battery life is a real 9 hours
rather than a marketing 12.

What does not: the phone app is required for anything beyond the two
hardware buttons, the app has no landscape layout, and the quick-release
plate is proprietary at $60.

Verdict after a month: keep it. The tripod-mode latch is the only part that
feels like it will break first.
