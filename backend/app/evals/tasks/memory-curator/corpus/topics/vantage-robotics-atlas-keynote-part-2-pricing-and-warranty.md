Second of the Atlas keynote notes. Subject continues from
[[Vantage Robotics Atlas Keynote]].

List price is $148,000 per cell, which Vantage compared against a two-shift
operator loaded cost of roughly $110,000 a year. Volume above ten cells is
"talk to us" — no published schedule.

There is a pilot program: one cell, ninety days, $9,000, credited against
purchase if you keep it. Jeremy's read was that the pilot is the actual
product right now and the list price is aspirational.

Warranty is 24 months on the arms and 12 months on the end effectors, and it
excludes the end effectors' finger pads outright — those are consumables at
roughly $400 a set and Vantage would not say how long a set lasts under the
carton workload they demoed.

Service is next-business-day on-site in five metros only; everywhere else is
depot, which means shipping a 240 kg cell.

The interesting omission: no uptime or availability commitment of any kind,
in a product being sold against a labour line.
