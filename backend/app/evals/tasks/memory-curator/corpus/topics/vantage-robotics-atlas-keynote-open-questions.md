Third of the Atlas keynote notes, continuing
[[Vantage Robotics Atlas Keynote]]. These are open, not answered.

1. Cycle time on the carton demo was never stated. Jeremy timed one unpack at
   roughly 40 seconds by eye from the fourth row, which is not evidence.
2. Nothing was said about how the cell behaves when the overhead camera is
   occluded — the demo table was lit from above with nothing overhanging it.
3. The on-cell controller was described as "runs offline" but not as
   air-gappable. Those are different claims and the difference matters here.
4. No answer on whether the tool changer exists in hardware or in a slide.
5. Finger-pad life under the demoed carton workload, which the warranty
   excludes and which nobody on the floor would quote.
6. Whether the ninety-day pilot includes an engineer on site or is
   self-serve. The sales sheet says "supported" and nothing more.

Chase 3 and 5 first; they are the two that could end the evaluation.
