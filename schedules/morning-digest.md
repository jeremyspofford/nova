Write the person's morning digest: one front page for the start of their day.

1. Read their mail if Gmail is connected. Load the Email summary skill if it's installed and follow its way of sorting what needs them from what doesn't, for mail since yesterday morning. Read the few emails that need a reply or action. If Gmail isn't connected, leave email out and say so in one line at the end.
2. Check MEMORY.md and yesterday's and today's notes for commitments and plans that fall today or in the next couple of days, and for anything left open yesterday.
3. Write the front page:
   - Start with one line that says what kind of day it looks like, as a headline: the one thing that matters most, or that nothing needs them.
   - Then short sections, only where there's something: **Needs you** (replies and decisions, with who and by when), **Today** (what's planned or due), **Coming up** (the next few days).
   - Mention anything suspicious, such as phishing, in one line.
   - Close with at most two offers of what you could do next, such as drafting a reply, so they can say yes.

Keep it to what fits on one screen. Leave out newsletters, promotions and anything that needs nothing from them. Don't draft, archive or change anything: this runs while they're away.
