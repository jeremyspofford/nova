---
name: standup
description: Turn what the user did, plans and is blocked on into a short stand-up update.
when_to_use: When the user asks for a stand-up, a daily update or a status note for their team.
---
## Instructions

1. Collect three things from the user's message: done, next and blocked. Ask only for what is missing.
2. Write at most three bullets per heading, each under 15 words.
3. Name people and tickets exactly as the user wrote them.
4. Write "Nothing" under Blocked when nothing blocks the user.
